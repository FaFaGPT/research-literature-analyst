# Multi-paper synthesis

## Input and evidence key

Curate 3–5 real papers addressing one scoped claim, with at least one challenge, scope difference, or incompatible measurement. Identify overlapping samples, duplicated reports, versions, and which results can actually be compared. Record source locations for support, challenge, and unresolved differences.

## Task prompt

```text
Use $research-literature-analyst to evaluate the supplied research claim
across these papers. Create independent paper notes, a Claim node,
and a discipline-appropriate evidence matrix. Explain the observed
disagreement, possible causes, supporting evidence, current interpretation,
and remaining uncertainty. Do not pool incomparable results or count
multiple versions of the same study as independent evidence.
```

## Acceptance and review

- Every substantive matrix row links to a source and location, with claim relation and comparison conditions.
- The synthesis explains why the group of studies supports, qualifies, or challenges the claim.
- Competing explanations and unresolvable differences remain visible.
- Apply alignment, coverage, synthesis, separation, and critical-analysis rubric dimensions; validate the vault.

Status remains not-run until a frozen source pack and actual outputs are recorded.
