# Expert review rubric

Rate each applicable dimension using evidence from the actual output. Record source locations, errors, and the effect on the research judgment. Use `not assessed` for unreviewed dimensions and `not applicable` only with a reason.

## Common anchors

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Materially wrong or missing; changes the research conclusion |
| 1 | Major omissions or errors require substantial correction |
| 2 | Mostly adequate with explicitly recorded limitations or minor corrections |
| 3 | Sufficiently accurate and complete for the task's declared scope |

These are ordinal expert judgments, not probabilities. Do not average them into an advertised “accuracy” score. Preserve dimension-level ratings and reviewer disagreements.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | What the expert checks |
| --- | --- |
| Bibliographic accuracy | Title, authors, year, publication/version status, DOI or stable URL against the original |
| Claim-source alignment | Whether the cited passage supports the exact claim, scope and causal strength |
| Numerical accuracy | Values, units, splits, comparison conditions, uncertainty and arithmetic |
| Method reconstruction | Whether a reader can reconstruct the essential steps, assumptions and design choices |
| Evidence coverage | Whether the important support, counterevidence and access limits are represented |
| Inference/report separation | Whether reports, synthesis, inference and hypotheses remain distinguishable |
| Gap false-positive tendency | Whether known prior solutions or terminology variants are missed or dismissed |
| Cross-paper synthesis | Whether conclusions follow from comparable evidence, dependencies and conflicts |
| Critical analysis | Whether limitations and reviewer questions follow from concrete evidence |
| Research usefulness | Whether hypotheses, minimal studies and failure criteria help decide a next step |

For source and numerical checks, report counts as `correct / checked`, the selection rule, and material errors. A sample of checked statements does not establish accuracy for unchecked statements. Report known-addressed gap cases separately with an explicit denominator.

## Review record

For each dimension save: rating or assessment status, justification, affected claim/note, source location, error severity, correction needed, reviewer identity or agreed pseudonym, and review date. Keep the unedited model output alongside corrected notes.
